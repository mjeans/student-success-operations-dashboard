# Decision memo

## Executive finding

The synthetic portfolio is broadly on track for activation and outcome
collection, but the average conceals a concentrated implementation problem.
Overall 60-day activation is 92.1% and follow-up completion is 89.6%. Five
schools meet the high-risk threshold, and three of them are in Summit Plains.

## What needs attention

Summit Plains School 4 is the first intervention priority. It misses five
thresholds: dosage, follow-up completion, support response, implementation
fidelity, and data freshness. School 3 misses four thresholds. These patterns
warrant investigation of implementation capacity and support operations,
rather than assuming a single student-level engagement issue is responsible.

There is also a predictable December participation dip across the portfolio.
Activity falls from 77.4% in November to 69.1% in December before recovering to
78.3% in January. A year-end engagement plan should therefore be standardized
across districts rather than treated as a local anomaly.

## Recommended actions

1. Assign a four-week recovery sprint to Summit Plains Schools 3 and 4,
   including staff-training completion, workflow observation, and weekly data
   refresh checks.
2. Review support staffing or escalation rules for Summit Plains, where fewer
   than half of tickets receive a response within 24 hours.
3. Prepare a November-to-January continuity playbook before the next academic
   year to reduce the December engagement drop.
4. Preserve segment monitoring. Grade, locale, and implementation-tier views
   should retain sample sizes and follow-up completeness beside outcome change.

## Translate the ranking into an action queue

Use [the intervention action queue](../outputs/08_intervention_action_queue.csv)
for proposed follow-up ownership, not just a list of high-risk schools. The
reference data contain 40 missed-threshold actions across 21 schools. These are
40 operating conditions to investigate, not 40 schools or confirmed causes.

For Summit Plains School 4, the implementation lead investigates dosage and
fidelity, the assessment lead reconciles missing follow-ups, support operations
reviews the response backlog, and data operations verifies the stale feed.
Its 50% training rate is context for the implementation lead, not a seventh
score component or proof of the cause of low dosage or fidelity. Confirm the
feed before drawing conclusions from the other metrics.

Keep the full cross-district ranking in view: Pine Ridge School 2 ranks second,
ahead of Summit Plains School 3. Its four flags should not be missed by a
recovery plan focused only on Summit Plains. Use the suggested owners to agree
actual assignments and capacity; the exported cadence is an illustrative
starting point, not a promised response time.

The [action-queue documentation](action-queue.md) specifies thresholds, routing,
training context, sample-size handling and the proposed cadence.

## Interpretation limits

All data are synthetic. Score change is descriptive and cannot establish
program impact because the design has no comparison group. The risk score uses
transparent, equally weighted thresholds and should be treated as a triage
device rather than a validated prediction. Suggested actions and owners have
not been validated as interventions or assigned to actual people.
